# Teleport

The Teleport tab lets you move a car to any saved location or built-in spawn point instantly, without restarting the game. You can also rotate the car in place and enable a continuous rotation mode for turntable-style shots.

### Car selector

Use the **car dropdown** at the top to choose which car to teleport. All cars currently loaded in the session are listed.

### Saved locations

Your custom locations are displayed as **thumbnail tiles**. Click any tile to teleport the selected car there.

To save a new location:
1. Position the car where you want it.
2. Enter a name in the input field and click **Add Location**.
3. Click **Save Locations** to write the locations to disk.

:::warning
Always click **Save Locations** before closing Assetto Corsa or Steel Studio, or your unsaved locations will be lost.
:::

Locations are stored in `[track_folder]/extension/steelstudio/locations.json` and can be shared with others.

### Built-in spawn points

| Button | Description |
|---|---|
| **Starting Line** | Teleports to the track's default starting position |
| **Hotlap Start** | Teleports to the hotlap spawn point |
| **Pits** | Teleports to the pit lane spawn |

### Rotation controls

| Control | Description |
|---|---|
| **+Z / −Z buttons** | Rotate the car by ±5° per click |
| **Flip Direction** | Rotate the car 180° instantly |

### Autoplay rotation

Enable **Autoplay** to continuously rotate the car around its current position — useful for unattended turntable shoots.

| Setting | Description |
|---|---|
| **Speed** | Rotation speed in degrees per second (1–360) |
| **CW / CCW** | Switch between clockwise and counter-clockwise rotation |
